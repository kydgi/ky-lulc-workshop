# Kentucky's Annual Land Cover Workshop

Tuesday, 2–4:30 pm, September 29, 2026.  
KAMP GIS Conference.  

In 2024, USGS released the first Annual National Land Cover Database for the contiguous United States. This database provides a consistent and comparable view of land cover across four decades. The Division of Geographic Information (DGI) has published every year of the annual land cover as web services and applications to help the communities in the Commonwealth access and analyze this data. This workshop will provide an overview of the data, how to access it, and how to apply visualization and spatial analysis techniques in an ArcGIS Pro environment. Finally, we explore to how use Jupyter Notebooks and Map Algebra to discover and measure landscape change.

**Skill Level:** Beginner to Intermediate.  

**Format:** Hands-on workshop with demonstrations and exercises. A computer lab with ArcGIS Pro will be provided for participants. Participants are encouraged to bring their own laptops with ArcGIS Pro installed if they prefer.

Boyd began mapping the hills and hollers of Kentucky in the 1990s through outrageGIS mapping and created topographic guides for public forests and wildlands. He later served as a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Geography at the University of Kentucky, teaching GIS and cartography. He is currently the Assistant Director of the Division of Geographic Information for the Commonwealth of Kentucky.
